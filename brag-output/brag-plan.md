# Brag plan: Group Portfolio Copilot (Al Hail concept demo)

## Answers
- **What it is:** an AI copilot for a holding company's leadership: one view of 12 businesses, questions answered in English or Arabic with every number linked to its source, and the quarterly board pack drafted for them.
- **Who it's for:** the owner and board office of a diversified holding company (here: a concept demo prepared for Al Hail Holding).
- **What sets it apart:** citations on every figure (and only confirmed ones), real Arabic right-to-left support, board pack drafted with sources.
- **Most impressive claim (from the product itself):** "Every number is cited to its source."
- **Visual hook:** the product's own headline, "2 of 12 businesses need attention this quarter".
- **Real UI shown:** portfolio cockpit → attention panels → copilot question and cited answer (English) → the same answer in Arabic → drafted board pack.
- **Tone:** `polished`: serious, elegant, long holds, soft transitions. Audience is an owner and a board.
- **Share caption:** "A portfolio copilot for a holding company: one view of 12 businesses, answers in English or Arabic, and every number traced to its source."

## Data and sharing
- All quarterly figures, borrowers and documents are simulated; business names and public facts come from alhail.com. The video keeps "Concept demo, simulated figures" on screen at the end.
- It shows a client's brand and a pitch built for them: share internally, or publicly only with Al Hail's agreement.
- No secrets, keys, hostnames or personal data appear (borrower IDs are fictional, e.g. SAMAA-C-017).

## Visual identity
The project's own: Al Hail navy `#062B49`, sand `#B0894E`, silver `#9A9C9F`, cool neutral `#F5F6F8`; Manrope (brand) and IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Real UI captured from the running app at 2x and animated (push-ins, pans, highlight rings, simulated typing) rather than redrawn.

## Music
100 BPM (beat 0.6 s, bar 2.4 s), A minor, soft and polished: warm pad, round bass, light kick and closed hat from bar 2, a sand-coloured pluck motif. Cut accents are soft filtered whooshes and a low tick, in the same reverb and key, under the music. Every cut below lands on the beat grid counted from frame 0.
